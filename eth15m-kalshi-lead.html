How Kalshi ETH 15 min settles

The target is the average of the CF Benchmarks ETH index over the 60 seconds before the window opens. The result is the same 60-second average before the window closes. Up wins if the result is above the target. Checked three times: for 10:00 AM ET this page computed 2,707.4435 and Kalshi posted 2,707.4447; for 10:15, 2,701.6018 against 2,701.6038. With 3 seconds left in the 10:15 window, the projected result was 2,678.036 and the settlement came in at 2,678.032. The index comes from Kalshi's public price file, the one its own chart reads. In each window's final minute that file's per-second series switches to the forming average, so this page rebuilds the raw index from the averages, exact to 0.0004 dollars.

What would make it an edge

A few seconds' lead only pays if Kalshi's quotes are slower than the perp. Kalshi's market makers can buy the index directly at one to five updates a second, without the public file's delay, and most watch perps. Whether the Up and Down prices lag can't be measured without Kalshi order-book data, which needs an API key. The model and the fee column show whether a typed ask leaves room after the fee.
