What the data says

180 days to 30 Sep 2026 · 17,180 windows · 5,199 official Polymarket settlements

One variable does nearly all the work: how far the oracle price sits from the price to beat, measured against how much it can still move. Everything else that looks predictive on its own, such as momentum, trade flow and Bitcoin, mostly restates that the price has already moved. Against a market quote at least as fresh as the model's price, the model matched the market's accuracy and lost money to the fee.

The model

P(Up) = T4(a × z + c × h). z is the log gap between the expected settlement average and the price to beat, divided by volatility × √(seconds left − 40); the 40 s accounts for settlement on a 60-second average, which ends 3 s before the close. Inside that minute the seconds already averaged are locked in. a rises from 1.19 at minute 1 to 1.59 at minute 13. h is the last-hour return in volatility units and c = −0.095, fading to zero by minute 14. Volatility is a 20-minute exponential average of squared one-minute returns, raised at once when the current minute moves more than that. T4 is the Student-t curve with 4 degrees of freedom.
