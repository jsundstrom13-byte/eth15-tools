Live fair value for the 15-minute Up/Down markets. Each tool connects to the price feeds from your own browser.

SOL KalshiKalshi SOL 15 min. Same engine; cleanest returns (lowest jump risk) of the four. BTC KalshiKalshi BTC 15 min. Least volatile and by far the most liquid, but the most late reversals.
